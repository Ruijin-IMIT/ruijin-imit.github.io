The challenge comprised two complementary tracks. Participants were encouraged to use public foundation models and domain-adaptation techniques, and to explore joint optimisation, multi-task learning or modular two-stage pipelines. A total of 80 teams from 12 countries registered, and 32 teams submitted valid results.

Task 1 — Deep gray matter nuclei segmentation

Goal: automatically delineate Parkinson's disease-related deep gray matter (DGM) nuclei from multimodal MRI.

Input: co-registered T1-weighted, QSM and NM-MRI images. Output: bilateral masks for the target structures. The QSM reference annotation contains 16 labels (eight nuclei × two hemispheres); the NM-MRI reference annotation contains the substantia nigra (SN/SNc).

Evaluation

Dice similarity coefficient (DSC) and 95th-percentile Hausdorff distance (HD95) were computed for each region. The composite score combined QSM (60%) and NM-MRI (40%), with Dice weighted 0.7 and Hausdorff distance 0.3. Region weights emphasized clinically critical small nuclei (SN 1.5, STN 1.2, RN 1.1 on QSM; SN 2.0 on NM-MRI). Higher is better; the theoretical maximum is 9.8.

CompositeScore = 0.6 × QSM_Score + 0.4 × NM_Score
QSM_Score = 1.0×CN + 1.0×Put + 1.0×GP + 1.2×STN + 1.5×SN + 1.1×RN + 1.0×DN
NM_Score = 2.0×SN
RegionScore = 0.7×Dice + 0.3×(1 − HD95 / 10)

The full scoring description is retained in the submission archive; per-region results are on the detailed results page.

Task 2 — Parkinson's disease classification

Goal: classify each subject as Parkinson's disease (PD) or healthy control (HC) from multimodal MRI.

Input: T1-weighted, QSM and NM-MRI images, with optional use of segmentation masks or imaging-derived features. Output: a binary label and, optionally, a PD probability.

Evaluation

The primary ranking metric was accuracy (Acc) on the held-out test set. F1-score and the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC) were also computed. Accuracy and AUROC were ranked separately during the design phase; the final ranking used test accuracy.

The segmentation and classification tasks are synergistic: accurate delineation of the SN, STN and other DGM structures enables precise quantification of volume, signal intensity, iron content and neuromelanin concentration, which in turn informs diagnosis. Four teams ranked in the top tier of both tracks.

Baselines and evaluation code

The organisers released RRMediCa as a PD classification baseline (63% validation accuracy, 62% test accuracy), together with a complete baseline implementation for both tasks, a Docker creation guide and prediction requirements.

Submission (closed)

Final evaluation used Docker containers to ensure reproducibility. The submission system closed on 31 August 2025; prize-winning teams' Docker submissions were verified by the organisers in September 2025. The submission format and evaluation protocol remain available in the submission archive.
